# LMX Inference Router — Daily Demand Seeding Checklist

**Goal:** 20+ daily active builders/agents using the router. Track two numbers every day: **new trials started** and **returning users** (returns are the real signal — trials just mean the credit worked).

---

## Daily tasks (do all 4, every day)

**1. Warm outreach — 5 touches/day**
Message people who already know LMX (Penn State network, past consulting clients, prior LMX contacts). Personal, not a template. Offer credits, ask them to run one real call through the router and tell you what broke.

**2. Cold outreach — 10 touches/day**
Reply/DM/comment in target communities. Lead with a specific use case or benchmark, not "check out my product." Don't spread across everything — go deep on 3-4 of these, not shallow on all of them.

*Reddit*
- r/LocalLLaMA — hardware, quantization, model/inference comparisons. Highest-signal spot for a router pitch; benchmarks land well here.
- r/MachineLearning — more academic/skeptical. Good for credibility posts, not hard pitches.
- r/ArtificialIntelligence, r/OpenAI — lower signal, bigger reach, fine for volume.

*Discord*
- Latent Space Discord (swyx) — center of applied LLM-engineering conversation; agents/evals/RAG channels.
- r/LocalLLaMA's official Discord — hardware/quant/local-model crowd.
- Hugging Face Discord — open-source/fine-tuning builders.
- Anthropic Developer Discord — smaller, quieter, high-signal for people building agentic products on Claude.
- OpenAI Developer Discord — largest builder community, broad reach.
- Cursor Discord — AI-native dev workflow crowd, early adopters of new tooling.

*X/Twitter*
- Reply in threads from AI-engineering-adjacent accounts (swyx, simonw, and similar) on cost/inference/routing topics — don't just post, engage in replies.
- Watch and engage with Coinbase's x402 / agentic-commerce threads — this is your actual positioning, highest-intent audience.

*Forums*
- Hacker News — Show HN post, plus comments on active "LLM inference," "agent," "router" threads.
- OpenAI Developer Forum — API-focused devs, good for technical credibility.
- dev.to — active LLM-inference tag; commenting or publishing here reaches people writing about the exact problem you solve.

*Agentic-commerce / agent-framework (niche, highest intent)*
- x402 Bazaar / Agentic.Market community channels — builders integrating with the same rails you are.
- ElizaOS community (Discord/GitHub) — agent framework builders; becomes a direct channel once the plugin PR lands.

**3. Community post — 1/day**
One piece of build-in-public content: a benchmark, a use case, a "here's what we shipped," or a comparison. Post to 1-2 channels. This compounds — outreach gets attention now, posts get found later.

**4. Reactivation — check yesterday's trials**
Anyone who used credits yesterday and didn't come back today: DM them. Ask what stopped them. This is more valuable than new outreach — it tells you if the product has a retention problem outreach can't fix.

---

## Weekly (not daily)

- Check x402 Bazaar/Agentic.Market listing status and MCP server discoverability
- Check ElizaOS plugin PR status (blocked on external maintainer, just monitor)
- Review the week's numbers: trials started vs. returned. If returns are flat while trials grow, the leak is in the product, not the outreach.
