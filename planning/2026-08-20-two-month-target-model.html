Explicitly not expected by 2026-10-20

  • Goal 1b — Filecoin/Arweave archival storage routing. Deferred by design once storage split into 1a/1b; not slippage if it hasn't started.
  • Goal 2 — the callable-function marketplace, unless the unprompted-payment trigger fires on its own. Not something to force.
  • Track B — onchain identity/reputation (ERC-8004-style). Stays behind the same gate as Goal 2.
  • L0/L1 — native DePIN compute supply / value-accrual layer. Directional signal only; not a workstream at any point in this window.
  • $5K–$10K MRR — the real 12-month revenue target. Two months in, "first real dollar" is the honest bar, not a run rate.
